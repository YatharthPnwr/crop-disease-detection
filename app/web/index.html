        The data

          How it works

          From lab photos and field photos to a heatmap

          1. 01

            Group near-duplicates

            PlantVillage has several photos of the same leaf. A perceptual hash links them, and the split keeps each group on one side, so test leaves are truly unseen.

          2. 02

            Add field photos

            Lab-only models scored under a third on real field leaves. We crop 8,688 single leaves from PlantDoc field photos, oversample them, and paste lab leaves onto blurred field backgrounds.

          3. 03

            Train two CNNs on Modal

            A 2.4M-parameter CNN from scratch and EfficientNet-B0 fine-tuned from ImageNet (head first, then everything at a 10x lower rate), three seeds each, in parallel on A10G GPUs.

          4. 04

            Explain and check

            Grad-CAM shows the evidence. A confidence threshold set on validation data turns shaky predictions into "please retake".

          Limits. Field accuracy is far below lab accuracy (see the model report), and 11 of the 38 classes have no field training photos at all. Knows only 14 crops. Disease notes are an unverified draft: check with an agricultural extension officer before treating plants.
